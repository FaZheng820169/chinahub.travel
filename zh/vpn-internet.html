Stay connected to Google, Instagram, WhatsApp and the outside world by bypassing the Great Firewall.

[中文] The Great Firewall of China

China restricts access to many foreign websites and applications. Without proper preparation, you will be unable to use:

Google (Search, Maps, Gmail) Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram, FB) YouTube & Netflix X (Twitter) & Reddit

[中文] How to Stay Connected

There are two main ways to bypass the firewall: using an International Roaming eSIM (no VPN required) or using local Wi-Fi with a robust VPN app.
