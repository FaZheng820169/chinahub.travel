Culinary Journey

Taste of the Middle Kingdom

From fiery Sichuan hotpot to delicate Cantonese dim sum - discover China's rich culinary traditions.

A World of Flavors

Chinese cuisine is as diverse as the country itself. Each region has its own unique ingredients, cooking techniques, and signature dishes that tell the story of China's long history and rich culture.

Eight Great Cuisines

🌶️

Sichuan

Known for its bold use of chili peppers, Sichuan pepper, and mouth-numbing "ma la" flavors.

🥟

Cantonese

Light, fresh, and steamed specialties, world-famous for dim sum and seafood.

🦐

Shandong

Seafood-focused with strong flavors, expert in quick frying and braising.

🐟

Jiangsu

Refreshing and elegant, with meticulous cutting techniques and precise heat control.

🍃

Zhejiang

Delicate with a preference for freshwater fish and bamboo shoots from the south.

🍄

Fujian

Famous for soups and seafood, with a focus on umami flavors from mushrooms and dried seafood.

🌶️

Hunan

Hot and sour, with generous use of chili peppers and vinegar.

🌿

Anhui

Focus on wild herbs, mushrooms, and stewing techniques from the mountainous regions.

Must-Try Dishes

🦆

Peking Duck

The most famous Chinese dish. Crispy skin wrapped in pancakes with scallions and sweet bean sauce.

🥘

Sichuan Hotpot

A bubbling pot of numbing, spicy chili oil broth. Cook your own meats and vegetables at the table.

🥢

Dim Sum

Cantonese tea house specialties - steamed dumplings, buns, and small plates in bamboo steamers.
