[Español] The 144-Hour Visa-Free Transit

The 144-hour (6 days) visa-free transit policy is a game-changer for tourists. It allows citizens from 54 eligible countries to explore specific Chinese regions without applying for a visa in advance.

The Golden Rule of Transit

Your itinerary MUST be: Country A → China → Country C. For example, USA → Shanghai → Japan is valid. USA → Shanghai → USA is NOT valid and will result in denial of entry.

Paper is Mandatory

Chinese border control often requires printed physical copies of your onward flight tickets and your hotel bookings in English/Chinese. Do not rely solely on your phone.

Eligible Regions for 144-Hour Transit

  • Shanghai / Jiangsu / Zhejiang: You can land in Shanghai and travel freely within these three regions.
  • Beijing / Tianjin / Hebei: Perfect for visiting the Great Wall and the capital.
  • Guangdong: Includes Guangzhou, Shenzhen, and Jieyang.
  • Chengdu: Visit the giant pandas.

[Español] Standard Tourist (L) Visa

If you plan to stay longer than 144 hours, or you don't qualify for transit policies, you'll need to apply for a standard Tourist (L) Visa at your local Chinese embassy or consulate.

Requirements generally include:

  • Passport with at least 6 months validity
  • Completed visa application form
  • Round-trip flight itinerary
  • Hotel reservations for the entire duration of stay
