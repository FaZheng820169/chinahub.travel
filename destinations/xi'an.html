The Cradle of Chinese Civilization

Xi'an, once known as Chang'an, was the capital of 13 Chinese dynasties over 1,100 years. It was the starting point of the ancient Silk Road and remains one of China's most important historical cities.

The highlight of any visit to Xi'an is the Terracotta Army - thousands of life-size clay warriors created to guard the tomb of Emperor Qin Shi Huang, the first emperor of China.
