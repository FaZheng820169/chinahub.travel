The Heart of China

Beijing, the capital of China, is a city where ancient traditions meet modern innovation. As the political and cultural center of China for centuries, it boasts some of the world's most iconic landmarks.

Walk through the red walls of the Forbidden City, marvel at the Temple of Heaven's stunning architecture, and hike the Great Wall at sunrise. Beijing offers an unforgettable journey through time.
