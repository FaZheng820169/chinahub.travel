The Heart of China

Guilin, in southern China's Guangxi region, is world-famous for its dramatic karst mountain landscapes. The city's name means "Forest of Sweet Osmanthus," and its beauty has been celebrated in Chinese art and poetry for over a millennium. center of China for centuries, it boasts some of the world's most iconic landmarks.

Cruise down the Li River past bamboo groves and water buffalo, bike through Yangshuo's countryside under towering peaks, climb the Longji Terraces at sunrise. Guilin offers an unforgettable journey through time.

Travel Tips

Make the most of your trip to Guilin with these helpful tips.

🎫

Book Li River Cruise Early

Li River cruise tickets sell out fast in peak season. Book 2 days ahead.

🚶

Stay in Yangshuo

The best views are outside Guilin city. Stay 2+ nights in Yangshuo for the full experience.

🌫️

Best Photo Times

Early morning on the Li River offers misty lighting. Late afternoon is best for Longji terraces.
