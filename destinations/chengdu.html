The Panda Paradise

Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan province, is world-famous as the home of giant pandas. Visit the Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding to see these adorable creatures up close.

But Chengdu is more than just pandas. It's a city where tradition meets modernity, where ancient tea houses sit beside skyscrapers, and where the spicy flavors of Sichuan cuisine delight taste buds.
