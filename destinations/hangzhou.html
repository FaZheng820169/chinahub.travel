The Heart of China

Hangzhou, the capital of China, is a city where ancient traditions meet modern innovation. As the political and cultural center of China for centuries, it boasts some of the world's most iconic landmarks.

Walk through the red walls of the West Lake, marvel at the Leifeng Pagoda's stunning architecture, and hike the Longjing Tea Plantations at sunrise. Hangzhou offers an unforgettable journey through time.
