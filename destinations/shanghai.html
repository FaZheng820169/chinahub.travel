The Pearl of the Orient

Shanghai, China's financial capital, is a city that never sleeps. From the iconic skyline of Pudong with its towering skyscrapers to the historic Bund along the Huangpu River, every corner tells a story of China's rapid modernization.

Whether you're exploring the ancient gardens of Yu Yuan, shopping on Nanjing Road, or experiencing the vibrant nightlife in Xintiandi, Shanghai offers something for every traveler.
