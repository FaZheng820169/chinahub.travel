The Rise and Fall of Dynasties

Journey through China's rich history, from ancient emperors to modern times.

A Civilization Unbroken

China is one of the world's oldest civilizations, with a continuous history spanning thousands of years.

Major Dynasties

1600-1046 BC

Shang Dynasty

The first historically confirmed dynasty, known for bronze casting, oracle bone inscriptions, and the development of early Chinese writing.

1046-256 BC

Zhou Dynasty

The longest-lasting dynasty, during which Confucianism, Taoism, and Legalism were developed. Also famous for the Spring and Autumn, and Warring States periods.

221-206 BC

Qin Dynasty

The first imperial dynasty, known for unifying China, building the Great Wall, and creating the Terracotta Army. The word "China" comes from this dynasty.

206 BC-220 AD

Han Dynasty

A golden age of Chinese civilization, known for opening the Silk Road, inventing paper, and establishing China's territories that are still influential today.

618-907

Tang Dynasty

A high point in Chinese culture and art, known for poetry, Buddhism, and the cosmopolitan capital Chang'an. Poetry flourished with Li Bai and Du Fu.

960-1279

Song Dynasty

Known for innovations in technology, including gunpowder, compass, and printing. An era of economic prosperity and Neo-Confucian philosophy.

1368-1644

Ming Dynasty

Known for restoring Chinese rule, building the Forbidden City, expanding the Great Wall, and Zheng He's famous treasure voyages to the Western Seas.

1644-1912

Qing Dynasty

The last imperial dynasty, known for expanding China's territory, but also marked by conflicts with Western powers and eventual decline.
