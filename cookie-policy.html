Cookie Policy

Learn about how we use cookies and how you can manage them.

Last Updated: April 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide useful information to website owners.

Cookies allow a website to recognize your device and remember information about your visit (such as your preferred language, font size, and other display preferences). This can make your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

2. How We Use Cookies

ChinaHub.travel uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly (e.g., navigation, access to secure areas).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Functionality Cookies: Allow the website to remember choices you make (such as language preference) and provide enhanced, more personal features.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used to deliver advertisements more relevant to you and your interests (e.g., Google AdSense).

3. Types of Cookies We Use

First-Party Cookies

These are set by ChinaHub.travel directly. They are used to remember your preferences and improve your experience on our site.

Third-Party Cookies

These are set by our partners and service providers (such as Google for Analytics and AdSense). These cookies are subject to the respective privacy policies of these third-party services.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. You can delete all cookies that are already on your computer and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed.

Browser Settings

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Opt-Out of Analytics

You can opt-out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Please note that if you disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly. For example:

  • You may not be able to log in or access certain personalized features.
  • Your language preference may not be remembered.
  • Some pages may not display correctly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last Updated" date will be revised.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
