# Pinterest Marketing Strategy for ChinaHub.travel
# Pinterest is the #2 traffic source for travel blogs after Google

## Pin Types to Create (use Canva, free):

### For Each Article, Create:
1. **Main Pin** (1000x1500px) — Title + one key stat/fact
   Example: "China Visa Guide 2026 | 53 Countries Can Enter Visa-Free"
   
2. **List Pin** (1000x2100px) — Numbered list from article
   Example: "7 Apps You MUST Download Before Visiting China"

3. **Infographic Pin** (1000x3000px) — Step-by-step visual
   Example: Visual timeline of Alipay setup process

### Best Boards to Create:
- "China Travel Tips" — general tips and guides
- "China Itineraries" — city guides, day plans
- "China Travel Prep" — visa, payments, packing
- "China Food Guide" — food articles

### Posting Schedule:
- 5-10 pins per day (use Tailwind or schedule manually)
- Best times: 8-11pm EST (US evening), weekends
- Join group boards about Asia travel, budget travel

### Pin Description Template:
"[Main benefit] | [Specific detail] | Click for the full guide → chinahub.travel"

Example:
"Everything you need to know about China visas in 2026 — 144-hour transit rules, L visa costs, and which of the 53 eligible countries can visit visa-free | Full step-by-step guide → chinahub.travel/visa-guide"

### Keywords to Use in Pin Titles:
- "China travel 2026"
- "China travel guide"
- "China tips"
- "Visit China"
- "China itinerary"
- "China on a budget"
