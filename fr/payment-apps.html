[Français] Surviving Cashless China

Cash is rarely used. Visa and Mastercard are rarely accepted directly. Setting up Alipay and WeChat Pay is mandatory for a smooth trip.

[Français] The Big Two: Alipay & WeChat Pay

China operates almost entirely on QR codes. Whether you're buying a high-speed rail ticket, a bottle of water at a convenience store, or renting a bike, you will use your phone to pay.

A

Alipay (支付宝)

The most tourist-friendly app. It allows you to link international Visa, Mastercard, JCB, and Discover cards natively.

  • ✅ Easy verification for foreigners
  • ✅ Built-in translation tool
  • ✅ Integrated DiDi (ride-hailing)
W

WeChat (微信)

The "super app". Essential for communicating with locals, guides, and scanning mini-programs at restaurants.

  • ✅ Essential for messaging
  • ✅ Restaurant ordering menus
  • ⚠️ Stricter foreign card limits

Crucial Tip: Set up BEFORE you arrive

Download, register, and link your credit cards while you are still in your home country. You will need to receive an SMS verification code, which is much easier to do on your home network.

The 200 RMB Rule (Foreign Credit Cards)

Both Alipay and WeChat Pay allow you to link foreign Visa/Mastercard. Transactions under 200 RMB (approx. $28 USD) are completely fee-free. For single transactions over 200 RMB, a 3% transaction fee is applied by the apps.

[Français] Other Essential Apps

🗺️

Apple Maps / Amap (高德地图)

Google Maps is outdated and inaccurate in China. Use Apple Maps (works great without VPN) or Amap (if you can read some Chinese).

🚄

Trip.com

The easiest app in English to book high-speed trains, domestic flights, and hotels that explicitly accept foreigners.

🚕

DiDi (滴滴出行)

China's Uber. You don't even need to download the standalone app—it is built directly into Alipay as a Mini Program and comes with an English interface and automatic translation with the driver.

🗣️

DeepL / Baidu Translate

Essential for reading menus and communicating. Pro Tip: Google Translate app's basic text translation actually works in China without a VPN!
